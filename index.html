How it works

  1. 1. Register

    The admin registers schools, students and partner restaurants.

  2. 2. Verify

    Only students confirmed as currently enrolled in a public school receive a coupon.

  3. 3. Eat

    Students show their student ID at a nearby partner restaurant and get a meal.

  4. 4. Reimburse

    Restaurants track every meal served and claim it back from the government.
